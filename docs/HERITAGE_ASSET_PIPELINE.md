# Iranian Heritage Asset Pipeline

## Purpose

This pipeline prevents visually attractive but historically inaccurate, unlicensed, or context-free landmarks from entering the game.

## Candidate-to-approved lifecycle

1. Candidate proposed with a stable content ID.
2. Research record created with official or institutional references where available.
3. Location, historical period, architectural type, and cultural context checked.
4. Image, drawing, scan, or 3D reference rights recorded.
5. Landmark reviewed by a subject-matter/editorial reviewer.
6. Art brief approved.
7. Blockout checked for silhouette, scale, and major proportions.
8. Materials, colors, ornament, and regional details reviewed.
9. Optimized asset measured on target Android device tiers.
10. Asset approved for a named game version and content revision.

## Required metadata

Each landmark record must include stable ID and localized names; city, region, and documented location; historical period and evidence confidence; heritage category and architectural description; primary source references and access date; reference-media source, creator, license, and attribution; rights holder or license evidence; art deviations introduced for readability; reviewer and approval status; and technical metrics such as mesh count, triangle budget, texture dimensions, material count, memory footprint, and LOD strategy.

## Historical fidelity rules

- Preserve defining silhouette and proportions.
- Use historically plausible materials and construction details.
- Do not combine elements from unrelated sites into a fake monument.
- Clearly label stylization where exact reconstruction is impossible.
- Do not invent a historic restoration story.
- Respect religious, funerary, and culturally sensitive contexts.
- If evidence conflicts or rights are unclear, keep the asset in research status.

## Licensing and provenance

A publicly viewable photograph is not automatically free to reuse. Record the actual license and permitted commercial use for images, scans, models, textures, fonts, and audio. Preserve attribution obligations. Do not rip assets from other games or websites. Do not rely on an AI-generated image as historical evidence.

## First candidate batch

The research queue includes Hafezieh, Saadieh, and Arg of Karim Khan in Shiraz; Si-o-se-pol, Khaju Bridge, and the Naqsh-e Jahan ensemble in Isfahan; Fin Garden and Tabatabaei House in Kashan; Yazd Jameh Mosque and documented historic urban architecture; Tabriz Historic Bazaar Complex and Arg-e Alishah; Ganjali Khan Complex in Kerman; and Golestan Palace in Tehran.

This queue is not an approved asset list. Verify every candidate and its rights before production.
